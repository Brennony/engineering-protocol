## Why I'm in Life Insurance
I got into it purely because it's commisions based, and I can make as nuch money as I really want to at a young age. It was, at first, just another sales job. But now that I've been in it for a while, I've realize the potential that this field has to change my entire life.

## What I'm learning from sales 
Sales is hard, but it's rewarding. Getting a sale affects me a lot right now, but I know that it shouldn't eventually. I've learned that persistency in working and doing anything is key. No matter what you do, you will fail. Failure is a part of life and it is important to accept it and learn from it. I've also learned how touch a whole day of rejection is, but I'm quickly learning how important it is to have those failures build up my toughness.
